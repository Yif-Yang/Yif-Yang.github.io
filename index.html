arXiv 2026Self-evolving agents★ 16.5K

SkillOpt: Executive Strategy for Self-Evolving Agent Skills

Yifan Yang†, Ziyang Gong, Weiquan Huang, Qihao Yang, Ziwei Zhou, et al.

A controllable text-space optimizer that trains reusable skills as external state for a frozen agent. Scored trajectories become bounded edits, and validation gates keep only changes that measurably improve behavior.

Best or tied-best in 52 / 52 settingsZero extra model calls at deployment
Research note +

Problem. One-shot or loosely self-revised skills do not behave like reliable optimization.

Approach. SkillOpt adds a textual learning rate, rejected-edit memory, held-out validation, and epoch-wise slow/meta updates.

Result. It improves across six benchmarks, seven target models, and direct-chat, Codex, and Claude Code harnesses, with strong cross-model and cross-harness transfer.

AAAI 2026Outstanding Paper★ 687

LLM2CLIP: Powerful Language Model Unlocks Richer Cross-Modality Representation

Weiquan Huang, Aoqi Wu, Yifan Yang†, et al.

LLM2CLIP turns a large language model into a stronger textual teacher for contrastive vision-language learning, improving alignment for long, dense, and multilingual descriptions.

300K+ Hugging Face downloadsTransferred to Phi-4-mini visual pretraining
Research note +

Idea. Caption-contrastive tuning unlocks semantic structure already present in an LLM, then distills it into a practical CLIP-style representation space.

Impact. The work received the AAAI 2026 Outstanding Paper Award and a WAIC 2026 Youth Outstanding Paper Nomination Award; released models have passed 300K cumulative downloads.

World-R1: Reinforcing 3D Constraints for Text-to-Video Generation

Weijie Wang, Xiaoxuan He, Youping Gu, Yifan Yang†, et al.

World-R1 aligns video generation with 3D constraints through reinforcement learning, improving geometric consistency and camera control without changing the underlying video-model architecture.

3D-aware rewardsPeriodic decoupled training
Research note +

Approach. A purpose-built text corpus, camera-aware initialization, feedback from 3D foundation models and VLMs, and Flow-GRPO jointly encourage structural coherence.

Result. A periodic training schedule balances rigid geometry with fluid dynamics while preserving the visual quality of the base generator.

arXiv 2026Agent evaluation★ 159

From Raw Experience to Skill Consumption: A Systematic Study of Model-Generated Agent Skills

Zisu Huang, Jingwen Xu, Yifan Yang†, Ziyang Gong, Qihao Yang, et al.

SkillLens studies model-generated agent skills across the complete lifecycle: experience generation, skill extraction, and consumption by a target agent.

Chendong Wang, Donglin Bai, Yifan Yang†, et al.

05 / About

My research connects multimodal foundation models, visual content generation, and general-purpose agentic systems. I care about the full path from an idea and a clean experiment to open-source infrastructure and real-world use.

I maintain long-running collaborations with research groups at Tsinghua, Peking, Fudan, Shanghai Jiao Tong, and Tongji universities, and regularly mentor graduate researchers across institutions.
