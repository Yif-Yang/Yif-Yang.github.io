NeurIPS 2026Self-evolving agents★ 18.9K

SkillOpt: Executive Strategy for Self-Evolving Agent Skills

Yifan Yang†, Ziyang Gong, Weiquan Huang, Qihao Yang, Ziwei Zhou, et al.

A controllable text-space optimizer that trains reusable skills as external state for a frozen agent. Scored trajectories become bounded edits, and validation gates keep only changes that measurably improve behavior.

Microsoft Global Hackathon 20261st Place · Executive Challenges Winner Award
Best or tied-best in 52 / 52 settingsZero extra model calls at deployment
Research note +

Problem. One-shot or loosely self-revised skills do not behave like reliable optimization.

Approach. SkillOpt adds a textual learning rate, rejected-edit memory, held-out validation, and epoch-wise slow/meta updates.

Result. It improves across six benchmarks, seven target models, and direct-chat, Codex, and Claude Code harnesses, with strong cross-model and cross-harness transfer.

AAAI 2026Outstanding Paper★ 693

LLM2CLIP: Powerful Language Model Unlocks Richer Cross-Modality Representation

Weiquan Huang, Aoqi Wu, Yifan Yang†, et al.

LLM2CLIP turns a large language model into a stronger textual teacher for contrastive vision-language learning, improving alignment for long, dense, and multilingual descriptions.

300K+ Hugging Face downloadsTransferred to Phi-4-mini visual pretraining
Research note +

Idea. Caption-contrastive tuning unlocks semantic structure already present in an LLM, then distills it into a practical CLIP-style representation space.

Impact. The work received the AAAI 2026 Outstanding Paper Award and a WAIC 2026 Youth Outstanding Paper Nomination Award; released models have passed 300K cumulative downloads.

03 / Foundation models

Pretraining & post-training, shipped.

Alongside published research, I am a long-term contributor to Microsoft's first-party models—vision pretraining, cross-modal alignment, agentic post-training environments, and reward systems.

Work below reflects contributions to shipped models and deployed engagements. Internal details are omitted by design.

Small language models

Phi-3.5 Vision & Phi-4-mini

For Phi-3.5 Vision I contributed the visual pretraining recipe, vision encoder and positional-encoding design, dynamic-resolution training, and mixed video–image training strategies. For Phi-4-mini I contributed the full visual LoRA pretraining and post-training pipeline, together with vision-encoder pretraining and cross-modal alignment. LLM2CLIP—our AAAI 2026 Outstanding Paper—was transferred into Phi-4-mini as its visual pretraining approach.

Agentic post-training

Next-generation MAI models

I build training environments, task suites, and reward systems for multimodal STEM, MCP tool calling, productivity documents, multi-document understanding, and deep research—then explore the matching SFT and RL recipes. A human-feedback-driven, self-evolving reward harness scores open-ended artifacts such as slides and web pages, and now serves the open-ended post-training pipeline as a preference-aligned reward model.

Sovereign deployment

Singapore HTX: localized small models

I led the customized small-model design and training in Microsoft's first strategic collaboration with the Singapore government—a US$10M engagement with the Home Team Science and Technology Agency (HTX). Coordinating a multinational engineering and product team, we built localized training and evaluation pipelines spanning multimodal capability, local knowledge, regulation, language, and public-safety requirements.
